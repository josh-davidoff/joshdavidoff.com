I connect platforms and people to enable a focus on mission.

Working at the intersection of grants management, process analysis & automation, and responsible, safe AI adoption, I help grantmaking teams translate strategy into approachable workflows and clean tech stacks. At the Hollyhock Foundation, I manage grant operations, build reporting and automation infrastructure across our tech stack, and translate complex organizational needs into systems for my team to use every day.

✳Core competencies

Process analysis

I map how work moves through organizations and redesign processes so that teams can operate with clarity and efficiency.

Systems & automation

I build API-driven automations and integrations that knit a tech stack together and reduce time spent on data entry.

Agentic workflow design

I design practical AI-assisted analytical workflows with human checkpoints and clear guardrails to keep users in the driver's seat.

Business intelligence

I organize messy operational data into decision-centered dashboards that show live progress on key priorities.

Change management

I support teams to adopt new systems with punchy documentation, up-front communication, engaging trainings, and plentiful opportunities for feedback.
