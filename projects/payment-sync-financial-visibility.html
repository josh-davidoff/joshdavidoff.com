Case study

Payment sync and financial visibility

Controlled payment workflows connecting Fluxx payment records to team trackers and Power BI dashboards, with a Bill.com invoice intake layer.

The problem

Payment data had to serve several needs at once, including day-to-day tracking, financial reporting, and budget visibility. Fluxx was the source of truth, but staff still needed easier ways to access live data they could sort and filter without relying on stale spreadsheet exports.

What I built

A controlled sync layer from Fluxx to Monday and SharePoint: Monday gives the team an operational view, and SharePoint Lists provides structured data for Power BI. A narrow set of fields, including state and due date, can update back through restricted paths, while Fluxx remains authoritative. An automated email layer sends invoices to Bill.com for processing.

System flow

What made it hard

A grantee will notice a sync error in payment data, so most of the design is about mitigating this risk and enforcing Fluxx's authority as the source of truth. When a record drifts, I built a defined way to fix it without having to edit in three places by hand.

What changed

Staff now check payment status in Monday and Power BI instead of an exported spreadsheet, and Fluxx is still the only place a payment gets edited.
