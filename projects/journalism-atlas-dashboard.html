Case study

Journalism research dashboard

A research dashboard that made a database of independent journalists and creators easier to browse and assess.

✳Screenshots

1 / 3

The problem

The program team had a long list of independent journalists and creators with just their names and outlets. This was not enough context to review the database with confidence. They asked whether I could fill in the gaps and make the list easier to browse and assess.

What I built

I built a local enrichment workflow and a single-file browser dashboard. The pipeline starts with creator names and outlets, runs bounded web searches, extracts evidence, and classifies each record by beat, creator type, geography, confidence, and review status. Source URLs, snippets, rationale, and review flags stay attached to each record so reviewers can see why a classification was suggested.

The dashboard embeds the merged 1,165-record dataset. A summary strip shows counts by beat and review status. Reviewers can filter and sort the table and open any record to read the evidence behind its classification. If they disagree they can correct the classification and leave a note. Records worth a second look get a star, and the annotated set exports back to CSV.

What made it hard

Every classification shows the snippet and the one-line rationale it came from. Low confidence is a real status that sends a record to a person rather than a guess that gets exported.
